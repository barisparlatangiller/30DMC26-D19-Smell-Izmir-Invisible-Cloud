    How to read this map
    1. Yellow haze is the modelled odour cloud near the ground: pale lemon where it is faint, sulfur yellow where it is strong, olive brown where it is densest.
    2. Moving streaks show where the air carries it this hour, from the sources (sped up).
    3. Pick a day and an hour. In summer the imbat blows from the north-west in the afternoon; at night the wind drops and the air slides down the slopes to the gulf.
    4. Click anywhere: odour now, odour hours that day, wind and the neighbourhood.
    Method, data & limits

    Wind. Hourly 10 m wind, temperature and humidity for the 21st of every month, Nov 2025 – Oct 2026 (Open-Meteo, one point in the gulf). On the map the wind slows and turns where it would have to climb a steep slope, speeds up over open ridges and the sea, and drains down the slopes on calm nights.

    Buildings. 323,318 OSM footprints. Heights from OSM tags where mapped (282), otherwise from GHSL GHS-BUILT-H 2018 (100 m). In the “with buildings” scenario dense, tall blocks slow the wind near the ground to 40–70% of the open value.

    Sources. Relative weights, not emissions: Çiğli wastewater plant (1.0), Harmandalı landfill (1.0), Meles mouth (0.6), Karşıyaka transfer station (0.3), six other stream mouths (0.3–0.5) and the shallow inner gulf, stronger when the air is hot.

    Model. Particles are released every hour, carried by the wind in 1-minute steps with turbulent spread, and fade over about an hour. The odour index is the particle density per 100 m cell, scaled 0–1 over all days. “Odour hour” = index ≥ 0.15.

    Limits

    This is a scenario: a way to see how wind, slopes and buildings move air. It is not an odour measurement, an H₂S map or a forecast. There are no public emission rates for these sources, so all sources use relative weights.

    One wind point for the whole area, a simple terrain rule instead of a flow model (no CFD), building heights partly estimated. The gulf's smell depends on water temperature, oxygen and algae, which are only proxied by air temperature here.

    Terrain: Copernicus DEM GLO-30. Trees are OSM trees, tree rows and points placed in mapped woods and orchards.
